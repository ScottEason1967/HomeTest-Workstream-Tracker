    20 JulData Sharing Agreements still paused behind the contracting trail; a session to be arranged rather than more email

    The Data Sharing Agreement position has not moved and is now the thing holding the DPIA. The 21 July board pack states plainly that NHS Legal is advising on the requirement for separate DSAs and logs the DSAs as dependent on PTT and NHS Legal advice and support, with the DPIA sign-off itself dependent on the MoU being in place with commissioners and the ACE contracts with suppliers.

    The programme position on the substance is unchanged. Data Sharing Agreements here are controller to controller, between NHS England as controller of the platform and the commissioners and suppliers as controllers for their own purposes, which is a different instrument from the data processing agreements the suppliers and commissioners hold between themselves.
